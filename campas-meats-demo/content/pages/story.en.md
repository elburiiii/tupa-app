## From New York to Salo

Campas Meats was founded by **Roberto Campas**, a classically trained New York butcher, together with his wife **Eveliina**. At the shop on Horninkatu in Salo, things are done the way a good New York butcher does them: whole-animal thinking, proper cuts, and someone behind the counter who can tell you where the meat comes from.

## Craft behind the counter

Sausages are made by hand. Steaks are cut from heritage-breed beef — *rotukarja*. Butcher's selections are put together from whatever is best right now.

## The farms we work with

Our meat comes from partner farms we know by name: **Vallan Maukas**, **Kiimalan Tila**, **Lurkin Tila** and a farm raising Wagyu-cross cattle.

## Where to find us

In the shop in Salo, at markets and pop-ups — including in Tampere — and online, with cold-box delivery anywhere in Finland.
